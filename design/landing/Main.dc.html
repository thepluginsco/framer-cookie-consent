Why Consentful

Most banners ask.
Consentful enforces.

A cookie banner that doesn't stop tracking is just decoration. We built the part that matters first — then made it beautiful and portable.

01 — Enforcement

Blocks trackers before consent. Not a cosmetic overlay.

Analytics and ad scripts are parked as inert code until the matching category is accepted — then activated in order, with Consent Mode v2 updated in the same tick.

Under the hood

Watch a tracker wait its turn.

From first paint to first "yes", Consentful keeps your site honest — and keeps Google's tags in the loop.

The consent timeline

Page loads — defaults go out firstAll four Consent Mode v2 signals are sent as denied before any tag can fire.
Trackers are parkedManaged scripts sit as text/plain. The browser never executes them.
The visitor decidesAccept all, reject all, or pick per category — and per vendor, when you turn it on.
Only what was allowed runsAccepted categories activate in order; everything else stays inert.
Choice is remembered — and revocableStored first-party with a consent receipt. A floating button reopens preferences anytime.
